Climate change is real!!

Every day, our choices in how we travel, what we eat, how much electricity we use and what we buy release greenhouse gases into the atmosphere. Together these emissions are known as our carbon footprint, and they are the main driver of global warming.

Do you know your carbon footprint?

Small changes add up. Understanding where your emissions come from is the first step towards protecting the planet for future generations. Find out how much carbon you produce and discover ways to reduce it.
